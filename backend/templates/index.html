Wiki-dle is a production portfolio game exploring reversed graph traversals and pre-computed distance matrices.

Created for Data Structures & Algorithms Prep. Wikipedia is a trademark of the Wikimedia Foundation.

How to Play Wiki-dle

Objective: Reach the **Target Wikipedia Page** from the **Start Wikipedia Page** in the fewest clicks possible.

1

Search and select outgoing links from the autocomplete menu. Blind guessing is disabled—you can only pick links that exist on the current article.

2

Receive **instant proximity feedback** on every click, calculated via reversed BFS on our server's pre-computed graph.

3

Proximity Indicator Guide:
🔥 Warmer: click-distance to target decreased.
❄️ Colder: click-distance to target increased.
◽ Unchanged: distance is the same.

Victory!

You reached the target page.
